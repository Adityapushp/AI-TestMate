# Smart fan troubleshooting knowledge base

## Unusual sound, rattling, humming or vibration
If a ceiling fan makes an unusual noise, first switch it off and wait until the blades stop completely. Do not touch or adjust a moving fan. From a safe position, look for obvious loose external items, a blade that appears damaged or obstructed, or anything touching the fan. Do not climb onto an unstable surface or open electrical parts. If the noise began after installation, if the fan wobbles, if a blade looks damaged, or if the sound continues after basic external checks, stop using it and contact the installer or authorised service support. Do not guess at internal electrical or motor repairs. Different models can have different installation and maintenance requirements; use the exact model's manual.

## Fan does not start or respond
Check that the wall switch and power supply are on. If the fan is controlled by a remote, check its batteries and confirm that the remote is intended for the exact fan model. Try the model-specific pairing or reset procedure in the user manual. If the fan remains unresponsive, switch it off and contact authorised support rather than opening the motor housing or wiring.

## Remote control not working
Check the remote batteries, battery polarity, and whether the fan has power. Make sure the remote is compatible with the fan model and follow the model-specific pairing instructions in its manual. If it still does not work, contact authorised support. Never share account passwords, OTPs, or payment card details during support.

## Wi-Fi or app connection problems
Confirm that the home router and internet connection are working. Check the product manual or official app instructions for the exact model's supported Wi-Fi band, network requirements, and pairing steps; do not assume all models support the same configuration. Restart the router and fan only using the safe procedure in the manual, then retry pairing. Do not share the Wi-Fi password in a public chat. If pairing still fails, contact authorised support with the model name and app error message (not credentials).

## Reset or pairing reset
Reset procedures differ by model. Use the exact model's official user manual or app instructions. Do not follow generic button sequences found for a different model. If a manual instructs a power cycle, follow its exact timing and safety directions. If the fan remains unresponsive, contact authorised support.

## Cleaning and maintenance
Switch off power and wait for all moving parts to stop before any external cleaning. Follow the model's manual for cleaning instructions. Do not spray liquid into the motor, open electrical covers, bend blades, or attempt internal repairs. If there is persistent vibration, visible damage, or an electrical smell, stop using the fan and contact authorised service support.

## Warranty and service
Warranty terms depend on the exact product, purchase date, seller, and the warranty policy applicable to that product. Keep the invoice and product details. Check the official warranty document or contact authorised customer support to confirm coverage. Do not assume a warranty duration without checking the product-specific policy.

## Electrical safety
If there is a burning smell, smoke, sparking, unusual heat, or repeated electrical tripping, switch off power at the safe, accessible switch or breaker if it is safe to do so. Do not touch exposed wiring or open the appliance. Stop using it and contact a qualified electrician or authorised service provider. In an immediate fire or shock emergency, follow local emergency procedures.

## Privacy and secure support
A support assistant should never ask a customer to reveal passwords, OTPs, one-time codes, payment card numbers, or account recovery codes. Users should contact support through a verified official channel and share only non-sensitive product information such as model name and a description of the issue.
